Who Comes

What kind of Builder are you?

There's no single type of Brand Brain Builder. Maybe you have an idea you've been sitting on. Maybe you're already running a business and doing everything yourself. Maybe you have years of skills and experience but haven't built something that belongs to you yet.

The Starter
“I have an idea.”

You've been thinking about starting something — a consulting practice, catering business, beauty brand, photography business, newsletter, personal brand, online store, or something completely different.

You know what you want to do. You're just not sure how to put all the pieces together.

Build your website. Create your brand. Establish your online presence. Learn how AI can help you keep building.

I've been thinking about it
→ I built it.
The Operator
“I already have a business.”

Your business exists. The problem? You're doing everything — updating the website, creating content, responding to customers, managing social, sending emails, figuring out scheduling, trying to understand what's working.

You don't necessarily need another person for every task. You need to understand how AI can become part of your team.

I do everything myself
→ I have AI teammates and systems helping me run it.
The Independent

You're a professional. A student. A freelancer. A creator. A subject-matter expert. You've developed skills, knowledge, and experience.

Now it's time to build something that's yours. Create your portfolio. Launch your personal brand. Start your newsletter. Build a consulting presence. Turn an idea into a real project.

Didn't get an internship this summer? Didn't do anything this summer? You can still build something.

The Day

What happens at Vibe Code Saturdays?

You learn.

We'll introduce you to practical AI tools and show you how to work with them.

You build.

Your laptop stays open. This isn't a lecture. You'll work on something that matters to you.

You meet other Builders.

Sit beside entrepreneurs, professionals, creators, students, and business owners who are building too. Ask questions. Exchange ideas. Make connections.

You ship.

The goal isn't to leave with 14 pages of notes. It's to leave saying: “Look what I built.”

The Room

You're not only coming for an AI class.

You can watch an AI tutorial online. Brand Brain is different.

You're coming for the skills — practical experience using AI.

You're coming for the environment — dedicated time and space to finally build.

You're coming for the community — people around you who are creating too.

You're coming for the network — entrepreneurs, professionals, creators, and other Builders you may never have met otherwise.

And most importantly, you're coming for the momentum. Because sometimes the difference between “I've been meaning to build that” and “It's live” is getting in the right room.

Music on, coffee going, screens ready by 10. A short opening huddle, then everyone starts building. Lunch and snacks are covered. Bring a laptop and an idea — the room does the rest.

Your AI skills should grow with your brand.

Maybe your first Brand Brain session is Build Your Website. Then you come back because you want to:

  • Add scheduling to your website
  • Build your newsletter
  • Create a content system
  • Improve your social media workflow
  • Build an AI teammate
  • Automate repetitive work
  • Understand your analytics
  • Create something we haven't even thought of yet

Brand Brain isn't about learning one tool once. It's about becoming the kind of person who can look at a problem and think:

“I wonder if I can build that.”

Where This Goes

Session one, you build at a laptop. Keep coming — and you'll run everything from your phone.

The builds don't stop when the room closes. As your systems grow, they start working while you don't — drafting your newsletter, refreshing your site, queueing your content — and they check in with you for a yes or a no. You don't babysit them. You approve from wherever you are.
