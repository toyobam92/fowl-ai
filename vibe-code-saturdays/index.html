What It Is

The goal isn't a website. It's you, AI native.

Most "learn to code" sessions end with a page. Vibe Code Saturdays ends with a teammate — an AI you've trained to run your brand, so the work keeps compounding long after you leave the room.

Building happens hands-on with a laptop, together, each session. Staying in touch with your AI teammate afterward — reviewing a draft, giving quick direction — works from your phone too.

Here's what that looks like in practice:

  • A real website, live on the internet, built in your own voice.
  • A newsletter you actually draft and send — not just plan to someday.
  • The beginnings of automations that keep both running without you starting from scratch every time.
The Room

The room is part of the product.

Music on, coffee going, screens ready by 10. Fifteen people walk in with fifteen completely different ideas. A short opening huddle, then everyone starts building.

You'll see someone lean over at the screen next to them — "wait, you built that?" — and start helping. Someone figures something out and passes it down the table. That's most of what happens in the room: people who've never met, making things side by side, then teaching each other what they just learned.

"By 2pm, you haven't just built something — you've spent four hours in a room full of people who make things. That part, an online course can't replicate."

Lunch and snacks are covered. Bring a laptop and an idea — the room does the rest.
