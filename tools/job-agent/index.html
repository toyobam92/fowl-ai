Set it up once. Then it applies.

Your AI job search assistant. Drop your resume in once and every morning it finds roles that fit, writes a resume for each one, fills in the application, and checks with you on Telegram before it sends.

How it works

About 15 minutes to set up. After that it runs every morning.

  1. Give it your resumePaste it or attach the file. It fills in your details and work history.
  2. Answer a few questions onceJobs you want, where you'll work, pay, work authorization. About 10 minutes.
  3. Connect TelegramTap a link and press Start. Matches and approvals come to your phone.
  4. It finds jobs every morningNew openings on company job boards (Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby) that fit your profile.
  5. It matches your resume to each jobA tailored resume for every role, saved in its own dated folder. It never invents experience.
  6. It fills in the applicationEvery question answered from your setup. Anything it isn't sure of comes to you.
  7. You tap Apply, or it applies on its ownTelegram mode asks first. Autonomous mode applies within the limits you set.
  8. It keeps your trackerCompany, role, industry, match score, stage and follow-up date, color-coded.

Two ways to run it

Start in Telegram mode. Switch to Autonomous once you trust its picks.

Telegram mode

You approve from your phone

Each match arrives on Telegram with the role, the match score and why it fits. Tap Apply or Skip. Two minutes over coffee.

Autonomous mode

It applies while you sleep

It applies on its own to jobs above your match score, up to your daily limit, and sends you a summary of everything it did.

Rules it never breaks

Never invents

No made-up experience, numbers, titles or skills. It only reorders and rewords what's on your resume.

Never guesses

If a form asks something your setup doesn't cover, that job comes to you instead.

Private by default

Voluntary self-ID questions answer "I don't wish to answer" unless you choose to share.

Stays in its lane

Never applies to your current employer or companies you block, and stops at your daily limit.
