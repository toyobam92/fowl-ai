Set it up once. Apply in minutes.

Your AI job search assistant, inside the AI you already use. Drop your resume in once. It finds roles that fit, writes a resume for each one you pick and fills in the application in one click. You check it and press Submit. Free.

How it works

About 10 minutes to set up. After that, about a minute per application. Focused: around 5 strong applications a day, not 100.

  1. Give it your resumePaste it or attach the file. It fills in your details and work history.
  2. Answer a few questions onceJobs you want, where you'll work, pay, work authorization. Each one is a tap.
  3. Connect Telegram (optional)Say "Connect Telegram", tap your own link and press Start. Steps below.
  4. It finds jobs that fitNew openings on company job boards, at your level, without duplicates. Run it any time.
  5. You pick from a short listUp to 10 jobs, each with a match score, why you fit and the honest gap. Tick the ones worth your time and tap once.
  6. It writes a resume for each oneTailored from your real experience, saved per job with a note on what changed. It never invents. Prefer your own resume for a job, or want something changed? Say so.
  7. One click fills the form, you press SubmitOpen today's applications on your computer, click Fill with Job Agent, check it, submit.
  8. You keep a copy of what you sentEvery job, its stage, dates and the exact answers you sent, on your job search page.

Two ways to run it

Start guided. Switch to Autonomous once you trust its picks.

Guided (Telegram mode)

You pick, it prepares, you submit

Your shortlist and reminders arrive on Telegram. On your computer, one link opens today's applications: one click fills each form, you press Submit.

Autonomous mode

It applies within your limits

On sites that allow it, it applies on its own to strong matches, up to your daily limit. When a site asks for a person at Submit (an emailed code), it sends you an Open & fill button instead.

Connect Telegram (optional, 1 minute)

Telegram is where your shortlist, reminders and "your turn" alerts arrive on your phone. Skip it and you approve everything in chat instead.

  1. Get TelegramFree on the App Store, Google Play or telegram.org. Sign up with your phone number. Already have it? Skip this.
  2. In your assistant, say "Connect Telegram"The Job Agent replies with your own link. It starts with t.me/FowlJobAgentBot and is only for you.
  3. Tap the link, then tap StartTelegram opens the FOWL Job Agent bot. It replies that you're connected.
  4. Back in chat, say "done"You get a test message on Telegram. That's it.

What you'll get there: your shortlist, a morning nudge when new jobs are in, an "Open & fill" button when an application needs you, and follow-up reminders. We never message anyone else for you.

Rules it never breaks

Never invents

No made-up experience, numbers, titles or skills. It only reorders and rewords what's on your resume.

Never guesses

If a form asks something your setup doesn't cover, that job comes to you instead.

Private by default

Voluntary self-ID questions answer "I don't wish to answer" unless you choose to share.

You press Submit

You see every answer before it goes. It never solves a "confirm you're human" check for you, and it never applies to your current employer or companies you block.
