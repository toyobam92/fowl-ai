How it works

Set it once. Never take notes again.

Recapp runs quietly in the background of the tools you already use. There are only three moving parts — and you only touch the first one.

1

Install & connect

Add the Chrome extension and connect your Slack workspace in about a minute. Recapp learns your calendar so it knows which calls to join.

2

It joins automatically

When a Zoom or Google Meet call starts, Recapp joins and transcribes in real time. No bot to invite, no link to paste, nothing to remember.

3

Slack gets the recap

Within 5 minutes of the call ending, a clean one-paragraph summary and action items land in the Slack channel you chose. Done.

Why Recapp

Built to end the “did anyone take notes?” problem

Otter and Fireflies give you a transcript. Recapp gives your whole team the takeaway — where they already work.

Posts to Slack, automatically

The whole point: nobody has to remember to share notes. The recap shows up in your channel on its own — so people who missed the call are still in the loop.

Summaries in under 5 minutes

Not a raw wall of transcript hours later. A tight one-paragraph summary with clear action items, ready before your next meeting even starts.

No bot to invite

Recapp joins your Zoom and Meet calls from the extension itself. No awkward “Fireflies Notetaker has joined” in the room, no manual invites, no forgotten links.

Never stores raw video

Recapp processes audio for the transcript and then discards it — raw video is never stored. Your team gets the notes without a library of recordings to worry about.
