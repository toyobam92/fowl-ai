## 1. Anthropic — Claude Duet, waitlist/limitations post

Post: https://www.threads.com/@claudeai/post/DexIVFjm5Mv/claude-duet-is-an-early-research-preview-available-through-a-waitlist-on-pro/

> Claude Duet is an early research preview, available through a waitlist on Pro and
> Max plans in the US. It can still misread a thread or misjudge what's urgent, and
> we'll keep tuning it based on what early users tell us.

**Our comment:**
> The 'misjudge what's urgent' caveat is the real story, not the waitlist. Muse, Dots,
> and now Duet all ship with the same disclaimer — triage judgment, not task
> execution, is agentic work's actual unsolved problem. How's Duet's escalation
> threshold different from Dots'?

## 2. Anthropic — Claude Duet, architecture post

Post: https://www.threads.com/@claudeai/post/DexIUFOm41n/we-designed-claude-duet-around-claude-opus-which-gives-it-the-judgment-to/

> We designed Claude Duet around Claude Opus 5.5, which gives it the judgment to
> decide what needs you, what can wait, and when to check with you first. It carries
> over the memory, skills, and connected apps from your Claude account, so there's
> little to set up.

**Our comment:**
> Carrying over memory, skills, and connected apps instead of a fresh onboarding flow
> is the detail knowledge workers will actually feel — most agent launches still make
> you re-teach context every session. Does that carry across devices, or just within
> one session?
